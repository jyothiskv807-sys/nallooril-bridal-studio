# Nallooril Salon and Bridal Studio

A minimal, editorial, and professional single-page landing website for **Nallooril Salon and Bridal Studio** located along Main Road (SH 59), Payyavoor, Kannur, Kerala.

## Design Philosophy & Identity
- **Aesthetic:** Ultra-clean, modern luxury editorial bridal salon.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Jet Black (`#0A0A0A`)
  - Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Warm Alabaster (`#F8F7F4`)
  - Champagne Taupe (`#C5B49E`) & Deep Bronze (`#5C5245`)
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: `Cormorant Garamond` (Serif editorial elegance)
  - Body & UI: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Crisp geometric sans-serif)

## Features
- Minimal sticky navigation with brand monogram logo and WhatsApp CTA.
- Asymmetric split editorial hero with 3:4 high-fashion South Indian bridal portrait.
- Three craftsmanship pillars (Signature Bridal Studio, Hair & Skin Expertise, Personal Attention).
- 2x2 Curated Services grid with hairline borders and baseline alignment.
- Visuals from the Bridal Sanctuary (4:5 editorial imagery showcase).
- Direct reservation cards (WhatsApp, Direct Phone, Instagram portfolio).
- Embedded clean grayscale Google Map view of Payyavoor with one-click directions.
- Strict direct conversion model (no cluttering forms, no review widgets).
- Mobile-first responsiveness with slide-out drawer and fixed mobile WhatsApp pill.

## Running Locally
Double-click `index.html` in any browser, or run:
```bash
node server.js
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

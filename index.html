About the Studio

Dedicated to refined aesthetics, calm elegance, and master craftsmanship.

Located along the Main Road in Payyavoor, Nallooril Salon and Bridal Studio was created for individuals who value personalized care, peaceful surroundings, and unhurried luxury. We believe beauty rituals should be calming retreats rather than chaotic parlour visits.

Our certified beauty specialists and bridal couturiers bring deep technical proficiency, medical-grade hygiene protocols, and an attentive ear to every appointment. From timeless South Indian bridal styling to precision haircutting and rejuvenating skin therapy, your individual elegance remains our sole focus.
